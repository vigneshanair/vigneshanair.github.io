Georgia State University · 2025 – now

A classifier that replaced manual photo review

Problem
Residents of Salvador, Brazil report sanitation hazards with photos, and every photo had to be reviewed by hand.
Built
With research partners, I co-built the data pipeline (collection, labeling, cleaning, preprocessing) across 4 neighborhoods and trained a classifier for exposed plumbing vs. open sewage.
Result
76% test accuracy from only 104 labeled images, consistent across all 4 neighborhoods despite changes in angle, distance and lighting. Extended to 5 classes and integrated into the MaisLugar reporting platform.
